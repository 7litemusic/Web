02 // What are Stems and why do they matter for my song?
Stems are the isolated audio tracks of the beat (kick, snare, 808, synths, guitars, etc.). They allow your mix engineer to sculpt the instrumental around your vocals, adjust individual levels, and achieve a radio-ready commercial sound.
